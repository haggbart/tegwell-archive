# Direct Heat

Step into the new age of localized direct heat solutions with Tegwell. From individual households to industrial complexes, we're shifting the paradigm from centralized to localized heat supply—enabling unprecedented efficiency and customization.

## Hyper-Local Efficiency

Say goodbye to the inefficiencies of long-distance heat supply. Our advanced geothermal systems deliver heat directly where it's needed, minimizing energy loss and maximizing efficiency. Around 80% of residential electricity use is for heat and cooling where direct heat is much more suitable and efficient.

## Tailored to Your Needs

Our technology isn't one-size-fits-all; it's customizable to fit specific heat requirements, from the home environment to the factory floor. Experience truly tailored heating solutions, adapted to your unique needs.

## Economical & Eco-Friendly

Embrace both economic and ecological benefits. By producing heat at the point of use, our technology drastically cuts both costs and emissions.
