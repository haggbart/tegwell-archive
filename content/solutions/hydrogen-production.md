# Hydrogen Production

Our emerging geothermal well and thermoelectric technologies have the potential to revolutionize hydrogen production and e-fuels. Locally produced and stored hydrogen mitigates the issues with remote production, transportation and storage of hydrogen, which is the biggest hurdle for hydrogen adoption in the world today. Leveraging High-Temperature Hydrogen Electrolysis, we aim to facilitate efficient, localized hydrogen generation for the future.

## Potential Applications:

- **Hydrogen Filling Stations:** Imagine strategically-placed hydrogen stations along major highways, fueling the green transportation of tomorrow.
- **EV Charging Stations:** These could sit alongside hydrogen stations, maximizing the efficient use of both heat and electricity generated on-site.
- **Infrastructure Advantages:** Our envisioned technology could offer heating and base load power at key points, enhancing the infrastructure along these routes.
- **Industrial and Dockside Potential:** Picture our units in industrial zones or ports, streamlining hydrogen supply and consumption right where it's needed.
