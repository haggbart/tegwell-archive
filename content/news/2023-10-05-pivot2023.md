# PIVOT2023

## Tegwell is a Finalist in the PIVOT2023 Startup Competition

Project Innerspace is a non-profit that aims to promote and transition our energy mix to geothermal sources. A key aim of Project Innerspace is to pivot technologies and resources from the oil and gas industry to the geothermal sector. Tegwell Tech was selected from all the applicants to be in the top five new geothermal companies working towards that goal.

## A unique and novel look at how energy can be extracted from geothermal sources

Tegwell has been noticed for its innovative techniques for accessing and utilizing geothermal energy. We see sustainability as a major pillar in our energy future. Creating closed-loop systems provides decades-long geothermal energy to future generations. Seeing many other companies and people excited to work for a better future is exciting.
