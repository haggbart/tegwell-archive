# About Us

## Who We Are

![](../../images/office_team.png)

We're a team of dedicated scientists, engineers, and visionaries committed to redefining the future of renewable energy. Tegwell is not just another energy company; we're a think tank of innovation, continuously working on groundbreaking technologies that have the potential to reshape how energy is produced, stored, and distributed.

## Our Vision

![](../../images/little_girl_hope_light.png)

Our vision is a world where communities can be energy-independent yet interconnected, producing more than they consume and sharing the surplus. We strive to create what we call "Surplus Havens" — communities that not only sustain themselves but contribute to the energy needs of surrounding areas.

## Our Journey

![](../../images/greenhouses-1.png)

From lab prototypes to real-world applications, our journey is a narrative of relentless pursuit for innovation. Though our technologies are still in the development phase, we are actively seeking partnerships and investments to accelerate their transition from concepts to transformative solutions.
